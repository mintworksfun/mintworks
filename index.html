About
Independent memecoin studio

What is MintWorks?

MintWorks is an independent memecoin studio launching coins and crypto tools with a strict commitment to transparency. Every project here is documented publicly from day one — launches, updates, positions, and mistakes alike.

The studio operates without outside capital or backroom allocations. Holdings and creator fees are disclosed, and nothing on this site or its channels constitutes financial advice.

The goal is simple: build memecoins that respect holders, document everything in public, and let the track record speak for itself over time.

How We Operate

1

Full Transparency

All holdings, entry points, and creator fees are disclosed publicly. No hidden wallets, no undisclosed allocations.

2

Ship and Iterate in Public

Every launch, update, and pivot is documented on X in real time. Wins and setbacks are shared equally.

3

Aligned Incentives

The studio holds positions in every project it builds. When the community wins, we win — and vice versa.

4

Community-First Design

Projects are structured so value accrues to holders, not just to the deployer. Creator fees are disclosed and fair.
